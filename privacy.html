GN Finance — Privacy policy

GN Finance is a set of internal finance tools used by Good Nature Supplies and Vaquero Hospitality staff. It is not offered to the public.

What we access

When you sign in with Google, the apps read your email address to check that you are on the list of approved users, and they read and write the company's own Google Sheets and Drive files that hold the finance data.

What we store

Entries you make (for example cash, cheque and bank records) are stored in the company's Google Sheets together with your email address and the time of the change. We do not read your personal Drive files, email or contacts.

Sharing

Your data is not sold or shared with anyone outside the company. It is only visible to approved users of the apps.

Contact

For questions or to have your access removed, contact Sherif Sawalhy at s.sawalhy@vaquerohospitality.com.
